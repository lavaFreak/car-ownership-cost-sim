**Channel concept:** Rapid-fire rankings & tier lists across pop culture — talk shows, celebrities, and basketball players.

**This episode:** "Ranking the GOAT Late-Night Talk Show Hosts"
**Runtime target:** 38–45 seconds (Shorts sweet spot)
**Aspect ratio:** 9:16 vertical
**Format:** Tier-list reveal (S / A / B) with a controversial bait pick

---

## The 3-second hook (CRITICAL)

> Most Shorts are won or lost in the first 2 seconds. Open mid-action, no intro.

**On screen (0:00–0:03):**
- Big bold caption slams in: **"This ranking will start a FIGHT 👇"**
- Behind it: a blank S/A/B tier board already visible so viewers know the payoff
- VO (fast, energetic): *"I'm ranking the greatest late-night hosts of all time, and number one is NOT who you think."*

## Engagement engineering

- **The bait pick:** Putting a beloved current host (Fallon) low is the comment-driver. Pick ONE safe-but-spicy demotion per video.
- **The open loop:** Leave one tier slot empty with a "?" so people comment their answer.
- **Direct question close:** "Who's actually S-tier?" — questions out-perform "like & subscribe."
- **Pinned comment (post immediately):** "Be honest — is Fallon B-tier or am I crazy? 👇" to seed the thread.

## Production notes

- **Pacing:** Cut on every sentence. No shot longer than ~2.5s.
- **Captions:** Burned-in, word-by-word highlight (karaoke style). ~85% of Shorts watched muted.
- **Music:** Trending upbeat instrumental at low volume; check the Shorts "trending audio" tab the day you post for a reach boost.
- **Images:** Use licensed/royalty-free promo or editorial stills, or your own commentary footage. Keep clips short and transformative (commentary/ranking) to stay fair-use friendly — see notes below.
- **Color:** Tier rows in classic colors (S = gold/red, A = orange, B = yellow) for instant readability.

## Reusable template for the channel

Every episode follows the same skeleton — only the topic swaps:

1. **Hook (0–3s):** "Ranking [TOPIC] and #1 isn't who you think."
2. **Lead with consensus (S-tier):** an undeniable pick to build trust.
3. **Add a 'robbed' pick (A-tier):** earns nods from real fans.
4. **The bait demotion (B-tier):** one spicy, defensible hot take = comments.
5. **Open-loop close:** leave a blank slot, ask the audience to fill it.

**Topic bank to rotate across the three pillars:**
- *Talk shows:* best interviewers, funniest viral moments, worst host exits, best couch chemistry
- *Celebrities:* most overexposed, best career comebacks, most underrated actors, biggest red-carpet glow-ups
- *Basketball players:* best dunkers ever, most clutch, most overrated All-Stars, best trash-talkers, top point guards

> **Series tip:** Number your videos ("Late-Night Hosts: Tier List #1") so each topic becomes a repeatable franchise viewers binge.

## ⚠️ Rights & compliance checklist (read before posting)

- Use **your own footage** or **properly licensed/royalty-free** images and clips; don't rip copyrighted broadcast video wholesale.
- Commentary/ranking adds transformative value, but fair use is **not** guaranteed — keep third-party clips short and central to your commentary.
- Use trending audio **from YouTube's in-app library** to stay within music licensing.
- Add visible **credit/attribution** for stills where required by the license.
